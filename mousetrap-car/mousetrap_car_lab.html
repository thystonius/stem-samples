Important model assumption: This version estimates string pull using the straight-line displacement of the bail tip (the chord of its circular motion), not the arc length. That is, s = 2L sin(θ/2). A real mousetrap car's string payout is still more complex because it depends on the changing geometry between the bail tip and the axle. The model also ignores friction, slipping, spring torque changes, and the car's momentum after the string goes slack. Use this to explore the ratios, not to predict measured race distance precisely.
